Vector game display data and icons

Aircraft and map names, UI icons, operator/nation metadata and country flags are extracted
War Thunder game resources, accessed through the public War-Thunder-Datamine
repository. War Thunder and its game assets belong to Gaijin Games Kft.
Vector is an unofficial companion and is not affiliated with Gaijin.

Source: https://github.com/gszabi99/War-Thunder-Datamine
